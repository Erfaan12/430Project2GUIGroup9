# Diagram Workspace

Place the final state-transition diagram and any drafts in this folder.

Recommended filenames:

- `fsm-draft-login.png`
- `fsm-draft-client.png`
- `fsm-draft-salesclerk.png`
- `fsm-draft-manager.png`
- `warehouse-gui-fsm-final.png`

The final diagram should show one initial transition to Login, role-based transitions after successful authentication, failure loops, all major sub-panels, delegated-role transitions, and the correct Back, Quit, and Logout destinations.
